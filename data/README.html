CamPrompt Annotations for the MultiCamVideo Dataset (v1.0)

Text annotations released with the WACV 2027 paper CamPrompt: Camera-Controlled Video-to-Video Generation from Text Prompt.

These files add natural-language video captions and dense camera-motion prompts to every training video of the MultiCamVideo dataset introduced by ReCamMaster. They are the annotations used to train CamPrompt.

No videos are included. Download the videos and camera extrinsics from the original dataset; the annotations join to it by file path.

Contents

File Rows Columns
captions.csv 136,000 file_name, caption
camera_prompts.csv 136,000 file_name, segment_1, segment_2, segment_3, segment_4
  • file_name is the video path relative to the root of the MultiCamVideo dataset, e.g. train/f18_aperture10/scene1/videos/cam01.mp4. Both files cover exactly the same 136,000 videos: 4 camera settings × 3,400 scenes × 10 cameras (the full train split). The val split is not annotated.
  • caption is a paragraph describing the scene, subjects and action of the video (median 82 words).
  • segment_1 … segment_4 describe the camera motion during four consecutive segments (quarters) of the 81-frame clip, in the camera's own frame of reference (median 7 words each), e.g. Move left while pushing in and turning right.

Example rows:

file_name,segment_1,segment_2,segment_3,segment_4
train/f35_aperture2.4/scene26/videos/cam02.mp4,Tilt downwards.,Tilt downwards.,Tilt downwards.,Tilt downwards.
train/f35_aperture2.4/scene26/videos/cam04.mp4,Move downward and forward while tilting upwards.,Move downward and forward while tilting upwards.,Move downward while tilting upwards.,Move downward while tilting upwards.

Usage

import pandas as pd

captions = pd.read_csv("captions.csv")
prompts = pd.read_csv("camera_prompts.csv")
annotations = captions.merge(prompts, on="file_name")

# Path to the video inside your copy of MultiCamVideo-Dataset:
#   <MultiCamVideo-Dataset root>/<file_name>

How the annotations were made

  • Captions: each video was captioned automatically with Gemini 2.5 Flash.
  • Camera prompts: each camera trajectory (from the dataset's camera_extrinsics.json) was split into 4 consecutive chunks. For each chunk we computed the relative 6-DoF camera motion (translation and Euler angles) and used Gemini 2.5 Flash to describe that motion in a short natural-language instruction. See Sec. 3 and Appendix "Details of Training Data" of the paper.

All annotations are machine-generated.

Known limitations

  • Automatically generated text can contain errors, including hallucinated details in captions and imprecise motion descriptions.
  • 49 source videos in MultiCamVideo are entirely black; their captions say so (e.g. "The video is a black screen.").
  • Segments split each clip uniformly in time; a motion change inside a segment is summarized in one phrase.
